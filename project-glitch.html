GLITCH

Sajt agencije za video produkciju i web razvoj

Website for a video production and web development agency

Pregled projekta

Project overview

GLITCH je kreativna agencija iz Beograda koja radi video produkciju, digitalni marketing, sajtove i aplikacije. Sajt je trebalo da na jednoj stranici pokaže sve četiri oblasti i da posetioca dovede do upita, bez lutanja kroz podstranice.

GLITCH is a creative agency from Belgrade working in video production, digital marketing, websites and apps. The website had to present all four areas on a single page and lead visitors to an enquiry without wandering through subpages.

Izazov

Challenge

Agencija sa četiri različite usluge lako završi sa sajtom koji ne kaže jasno ništa. Trebalo je da:

An agency with four different services easily ends up with a website that says nothing clearly. It had to:

  • Sve četiri usluge stanu na jednu stranicu, a da nijedna ne deluje sporedno
  • Fit all four services on one page without any of them feeling secondary
  • Proces saradnje bude objašnjen pre prvog poziva
  • Explain how working together goes before the first call
  • Se vidi sa kojim alatima agencija radi
  • Show which tools the agency works with
  • Put do upita bude kratak, sa izborom usluge u formi
  • Keep the path to an enquiry short, with a service choice in the form
