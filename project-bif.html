BIF - Balkan Influence Fighting

Sajt za prvi balkanski influenser boks spektakl

Website for the first Balkan influencer boxing show

Pregled projekta

Project overview

BIF je boks spektakl u kome se u ringu sastaju influenseri sa Balkana. Sajt je glavno mesto za sve oko događaja: ulaznice, borce, vesti i rezultate, prenos, prodaju dresova i saradnju sa sponzorima, na srpskom i engleskom.

BIF is a boxing show where influencers from the Balkans meet in the ring. The website is the hub for everything around the event: tickets, fighters, news and results, the stream, jersey sales and sponsor partnerships, in Serbian and English.

Izazov

Challenge

Ovakav događaj živi od hajpa pre borbe, a sajt mora da prati sve faze: najavu, prodaju ulaznica, samu borbu i rezultate posle nje. Trebalo je da:

An event like this runs on the hype before the fight, and the website has to follow every stage: the announcement, ticket sales, the fight itself and the results afterwards. It had to:

  • Ulaznice budu na jedan klik, odmah na vrhu
  • Put tickets one click away, right at the top
  • Svaki borac ima profil sa nadimkom i težinskom kategorijom
  • Give every fighter a profile with a nickname and weight class
  • Vesti, pravila i zvanični rezultati izlaze brzo, uz fotografije
  • Publish news, rules and official results quickly, with photos
  • Sponzori vide jasne nivoe partnerstva i imaju direktan put do saradnje
  • Show sponsors clear partnership tiers and a direct route to working together
  • Sve radi na srpskom i engleskom
  • Work in both Serbian and English

Odbrojavanje i ulaznice u herou

A countdown and tickets in the hero

The hero announces the next BIF with a ticket button and a countdown to fight night, with the trailer right below.

A carousel with every fighter: nickname, name and weight class, with a champion badge.

News and results

Sekcija za najave, pravila i zvanične rezultate, svaka vest sa datumom i fotografijom.

A section for announcements, rules and official results, each item with a date and photo.

Three partnership tiers

Main, premium and basic partner, each with clearly listed benefits and a contact button.

Prenos, dresovi i kontakt

Stream, jerseys and contact

Posebna stranica za PPV prenos, porudžbina zvaničnih dresova kroz formu, kontakt forma sa izborom teme i prijava na newsletter. Forme su zaštićene reCAPTCHA proverom.

A dedicated page for the PPV stream, official jersey orders through a form, a contact form with a topic selector and a newsletter signup. The forms are protected with reCAPTCHA.
