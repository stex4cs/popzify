RAA Team

Sajt agencije za hostese, promotere i talente, na tri jezika

Website for a hostess, promoter and talent agency, in three languages

Pregled projekta

Project overview

RAA Team bira, priprema i vodi ljude koji predstavljaju brendove: hostese, promotere, modele i brand ambasadore na eventima, snimanjima i u noćnom životu. Sajt radi dva posla odjednom. Klijentima pokazuje šta agencija nudi, a talentima daje mesto da se prijave.

RAA Team selects, prepares and manages the people who represent brands: hostesses, promoters, models and brand ambassadors at events, on set and in nightlife. The website does two jobs at once. It shows clients what the agency offers and gives talent a place to apply.

Izazov

Challenge

Agencija za hostese se lako predstavi jeftino. Ovde je trebalo suprotno, da sajt deluje diskretno i ozbiljno i da:

A hostess agency can easily come across as cheap. Here the opposite was needed: a website that feels discreet and serious, and that would:

  • Prikaže deset različitih vrsta angažmana, od sajmova do protokola
  • Present ten different kinds of engagement, from trade fairs to protocol
  • Pokaže stvarne fotografije sa setova i događaja, a ne stock slike
  • Show real photos from sets and events instead of stock images
  • Odvoji upit klijenta od prijave talenta
  • Separate a client enquiry from a talent application
  • Radi na srpskom, engleskom i ruskom
  • Work in Serbian, English and Russian

Solution

Deset usluga kao pregledna lista

Ten services as a clear list

Hostese i promoterke, spotovi i produkcija, eventi i lansiranja, korporativne proslave, casino i gaming, sajmovi i izložbe, modeling i kasting, brand ambasadori, nightlife i klubovi, protokol i VIP usluga. Uz svaku stavku kratko piše šta angažman podrazumeva.

Hostesses and promoters, music videos and production, events and launches, corporate celebrations, casino and gaming, trade fairs and exhibitions, modelling and casting, brand ambassadors, nightlife and clubs, protocol and VIP service. Each item briefly says what the engagement involves.

Galerija iz produkcije

A production gallery

Fotografije sa setova, događaja i VIP programa, pa posetilac odmah vidi nivo angažmana.

Photos from sets, events and VIP programmes, so visitors see the level of work straight away.

Proces u četiri koraka

A four-step process

Brif, selekcija, priprema i realizacija, svaki korak u par rečenica.

Brief, selection, preparation and execution, each step in a couple of sentences.

Dve vrste saradnje

Two kinds of collaboration

Posebni putevi za klijente i za talente, svaki vodi do svoje forme, pa se upiti ne mešaju.

Separate paths for clients and for talent, each leading to its own form, so enquiries never get mixed up.

Tri jezika

Three languages

Srpski, engleski i ruski, svaki na svojoj adresi i povezan hreflang oznakama. Osnovna adresa sama preusmerava na srpsku verziju.

Serbian, English and Russian, each on its own URL and linked with hreflang tags. The root address redirects to the Serbian version on its own.
