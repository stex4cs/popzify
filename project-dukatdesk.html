DUKAT Private Desk

Sajt za privatni desk za otkup i prodaju kriptovaluta, na četiri jezika

A website for a private digital asset desk, in four languages

Pregled projekta

Project overview

DUKAT je privatni desk za razmenu USDT, EUR i USD u većim iznosima. Nema samouslužne platforme ni korisničkih naloga. Klijent pošalje zahtev, desk mu lično odgovori sa kursom, i dogovor se nastavlja preko Telegrama, WhatsApp-a ili pisanog zahteva. Sajt je trebalo da taj model objasni na prvi pogled i da posetioca bez lutanja dovede do zahteva.

DUKAT is a private desk for exchanging USDT, EUR and USD in larger amounts. There is no self-service platform and no user accounts. A client sends a request, the desk replies with a personal quote, and the deal continues over Telegram, WhatsApp or a written request. The website had to explain that model at a glance and lead visitors straight to a request.

Izazov

Challenge

Privatni desk se ne prodaje kao menjačnica. Ko menja veći iznos ne traži dugme „kupi", nego nekoga kome može da veruje. Zato je sajt morao da:

A private desk is not sold like an exchange. Someone moving a larger amount is not looking for a "buy" button, but for someone they can trust. So the website had to:

  • Odmah objasni da nema samouslužne trgovine i da svaki kurs daje čovek
  • Make it clear right away that there is no self-service trading and every quote comes from a person
  • Uliva poverenje mirnim tonom, bez prodajnog pritiska
  • Build trust through a calm tone, without sales pressure
  • Radi na četiri jezika, sa verzijama pravilno označenim za pretraživače
  • Work in four languages, with versions properly marked up for search engines
  • Posetioca što kraćim putem dovede do zahteva, preko kanala koji mu je najlakši
  • Get the visitor to a request by the shortest route, through whichever channel suits them

Rešenje

Solution

Ponuda odmah u herou

A quote request in the hero

Na samom vrhu stoji mali obrazac: šta imate, šta želite i koliko. To nije kalkulator sa kursom, jer kurs ne postoji dok ga desk ne da. Umesto toga odmah pokazuje kako saradnja izgleda i vodi pravo na Telegram ili WhatsApp.

Right at the top sits a small form: what you have, what you want and how much. It is not a rate calculator, because there is no rate until the desk gives one. Instead it shows straight away how working together looks and leads directly to Telegram or WhatsApp.

Proces u četiri koraka

A four-step process

Zahtev, ponuda, potvrda, realizacija. Svaki korak u jednoj rečenici, da klijent pre prvog kontakta zna tačno šta ga čeka.

Request, quote, confirmation, settlement. Each step in a single sentence, so the client knows exactly what to expect before making contact.

Četiri jezika, svaki na svojoj adresi

Four languages, each on its own URL

Srpski, engleski, nemački i ruski. Verzije su međusobno povezane hreflang oznakama, pa pretraživač svakom posetiocu pokazuje onu na njegovom jeziku.

Serbian, English, German and Russian. The versions are linked with hreflang tags, so search engines show each visitor the one in their own language.
