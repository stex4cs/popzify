Correct Score Pro

AI platforma za fudbalske prognoze, na četiri jezika

An AI football prediction platform, in four languages

Pregled projekta

Project overview

Correct Score Pro je platforma za prognoze fudbalskih utakmica. Za svaku utakmicu računa verovatne rezultate kombinovanjem Monte Carlo simulacija, Poissonove raspodele i mašinskog učenja, i prikazuje ih uz kvote uživo. Prva verzija bila je sajt sa prognozama i pretplatom. Od tada je prerasla u pun proizvod: dashboard, analize, statistiku modela, kalkulatore, ligu prognoza i VIP članstvo, na četiri jezika.

Correct Score Pro is a football match prediction platform. For every match it estimates likely scores by combining Monte Carlo simulations, the Poisson distribution and machine learning, and shows them alongside live odds. The first version was a predictions site with a subscription. Since then it has grown into a full product: a dashboard, analyses, model statistics, calculators, a prediction league and VIP membership, in four languages.

Izazov

Challenge

Prognoze su pune brojeva, a većina posetilaca samo želi da zna šta će se desiti i koliko je to sigurno. Platforma je morala da:

Predictions are full of numbers, while most visitors just want to know what will happen and how likely it is. The platform had to:

  • Svakog dana prikaže mnogo utakmica iz velikog broja liga, a da ekran ostane pregledan
  • Show a large number of matches from many leagues every day while keeping the screen readable
  • Objasni odakle prognoza dolazi, a ne samo da je ispiše
  • Explain where a prediction comes from instead of just printing it
  • Ima posebnu stranicu za svaku utakmicu, koju pretraživač može da pronađe
  • Have a dedicated page for every match that search engines can find
  • Radi na četiri jezika, za publiku van regiona
  • Work in four languages for an audience beyond the region
  • Jasno razdvoji besplatan sadržaj od VIP članstva
  • Clearly separate free content from VIP membership

Rešenje

Solution

Dashboard sa današnjim utakmicama

A dashboard of today's matches

Početna je pregled dana: izbor datuma, filter liga, omiljeni timovi, utakmice koje tek počinju, uživo i završene, i kvote uz svaku utakmicu.

The home page is an overview of the day: a date picker, a league filter, favourite teams, upcoming, live and finished matches, and odds next to every match.

Stranica za svaku utakmicu

A page for every match

Za svaku utakmicu nastaje posebna stranica sa prognozom, formom oba tima, prosekom golova i međusobnim duelima. Adrese su čitljive, po ligi, timovima i datumu, pa se svaka utakmica može pronaći u pretrazi.

Every match gets its own page with the prediction, both teams' form, average goals and head-to-head record. URLs are readable, by league, teams and date, so each match can be found in search.

Četiri modela, prikazana javno

Four models, shown in the open

Stranica statistike pokazuje kako rade Monte Carlo, Poissonova raspodela, mašinsko učenje i kombinovani model, svaki sa svojom preciznošću.

The statistics page shows how Monte Carlo, the Poisson distribution, machine learning and the combined model perform, each with its own accuracy.

Kalkulatori

Calculators

Četiri alata na jednom mestu: value bet, Kelly kriterijum, očekivana vrednost i arbitraža.

Four tools in one place: value bet, the Kelly criterion, expected value and arbitrage.

Liga prognoza, kvote uživo i VIP

A prediction league, live odds and VIP

Correct Score League je besplatna igra u kojoj se svakog dana prognoziraju dve izdvojene utakmice. Uz utakmice stoje kvote partnera SpinBetter, a za one koji žele više postoji VIP članstvo.

Correct Score League is a free game where users predict two featured matches every day. Matches show odds from the partner SpinBetter, and there is VIP membership for those who want more.

Četiri jezika, svaki na svojoj adresi

Four languages, each on its own URL

Engleski, španski, francuski i portugalski. Verzije su povezane hreflang oznakama, pa pretraživač svakom posetiocu pokazuje onu na njegovom jeziku.

English, Spanish, French and Portuguese. The versions are linked with hreflang tags, so search engines show each visitor the one in their own language.
